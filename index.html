Six advantages of Aegis, and the proof.

  • Stable LFP chemistry

    Iron phosphate does not release oxygen when heated, so fire risk is far lower than with NMC.

    Passed the nail penetration test with no fire or explosion.

  • Three-layer battery management

    The BMS monitors voltage, current, and temperature in every module, and cuts power before safe limits are exceeded.

    Disconnect response under 10 milliseconds in short-circuit testing.

  • Active liquid cooling

    Cell-to-cell temperature difference is held to 3 °C at most, so cells age evenly and the pack lasts longer.

    Tested for 500 hours at 55 °C ambient temperature.

  • Flood and dust resistant

    A sealed aluminium casing rated IP67 for potholes, standing water, and the rainy season.

    Submerged at 1 meter for 30 minutes with no leakage.

  • Fast charging that protects the cells

    An adaptive charging profile lowers current when cells are warm so capacity does not fade quickly.

    92% capacity after 1,000 fast-charge cycles.

  • Recyclable

    A modular design makes disassembly easy, and 95% of materials return to production.

    A take-back program is available for corporate customers.

Frequently asked questions.

Is Aegis compatible with vehicles already on the road?

Aegis is available for several vehicle platforms. Send your vehicle's make, model, and year through the contact form and our technical team will check compatibility.

What does the 8-year warranty cover?

The warranty covers manufacturing defects and capacity falling below 70% for 8 years or 200,000 km, whichever comes first. Full terms are written into the contract.

How long do installation and regular servicing take?

Replacing the pack takes about 1 business day at a partner workshop. A check-up every 12 months is recommended and is included in the warranty package.

What happens to the battery at the end of its life?

Voltera takes back end-of-life packs from corporate customers. Modules still in good condition are reused for energy storage, and the rest are recycled.

Need a calculation for your vehicle?

We prepare a cost estimate and installation schedule once we know the type and number of units.
